About Dr. Tanvir Ahmed

Dr. Tanvir Ahmed is a dedicated Neonatal & Pediatric Specialist providing expert medical care for infants, children, and adolescents. His philosophy centers on preventive care, parental education, and holistic treatment.

He is available for consultation at Banshkhali Maa-Shishu O General Hospital Ltd., offering specialized pediatric services twice a week.
